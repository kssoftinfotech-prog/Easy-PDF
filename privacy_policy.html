Privacy Policy for Easy PDF

Effective Date: March 30, 2026 | App: Easy PDF - Document Scanner & Maker | Developer: KS Soft Infotech

1. Introduction

Welcome to Easy PDF - Document Scanner & Maker ("Easy PDF", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure experience when using our application.

2. Information Collection and Storage

A. Local Document Processing (100% On-Device)

  • Camera Access: Easy PDF requests access to your device camera solely to allow you to capture physical documents for scanning and PDF generation.
  • Photo Library / Storage Access: Easy PDF requests access to your photo library/storage to allow you to select existing photos for PDF conversion.
  • Scanned Files & Generated PDFs: All document scanning, edge detection, perspective cropping, image processing, and PDF creation occur entirely on your local device. Your scanned documents and created PDF files are saved locally on your device and are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on our servers or any third-party cloud servers.

B. Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

Our app displays rewarded video advertisements provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable data, including:

  • Unique Device Identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID).
  • IP Address (used for coarse location/region estimation).
  • App interaction, ad impression, and performance metrics.

For more details, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Third-Party SDKs and Services

Easy PDF integrates trusted third-party SDKs to provide core features:

  • Google ML Kit Document Scanner: Provides on-device document border detection, auto-cropping, and image enhancement.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Serves rewarded ads before PDF generation.
  • Google Play Services / Play In-App Review: Enables rating and reviewing the application on the Google Play Store.

4. User Rights and Data Control

  • Data Control: All your PDF documents reside locally on your device. You can view, rename, or delete any created PDF file directly inside the app or through your device's file manager at any time.
  • Permissions: You can grant or revoke Camera and Storage permissions at any time via your device's System Settings > Apps > Easy PDF > Permissions.

5. Children's Privacy

Easy PDF is a general audience utility application and does not knowingly collect personal or sensitive information from children under the age of 13.
